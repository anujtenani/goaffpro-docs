# Enable Marketing CRM for Affiliates

**GoAffPro** provides you with the option to enable the marketing CRM for your affiliates.

To enable the marketing CRM for your affiliates, go to the **For Affiliates** section in the **Marketing** tab of the GoAffPro admin panel.&#x20;

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (3913).png" alt=""><figcaption><p>Marketing > For Affiliates</p></figcaption></figure>

Here, you can configure the marketing CRM access. You can make it available either for all affiliates or for specific affiliate groups.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot 2026-07-15 151329.png" alt=""><figcaption><p>Configure marketing CRM access</p></figcaption></figure>

You can also view the monthly usage statistics for affiliate campaigns and sent emails.

<summary>FAQ</summary>

**Where do affiliates access the marketing CRM from their dashboard?**

Affiliates can access the marketing CRM in their dashboard from the Marketing Tools > Contacts section.&#x20;

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot 2026-07-15 151341.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

**What can affiliates do with the marketing CRM?**&#x20;

Affiliates can view and manage customers or leads directly from their dashboard. They can edit contact details and create new contacts. Additionally, they can create targeted email campaigns and send them to these customers or leads.
