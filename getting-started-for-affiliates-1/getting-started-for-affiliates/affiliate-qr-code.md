# Affiliate QR Code

Affiliates are provided with a QR code in their dashboard. The QR code when scanned will open the referral link of the affiliate.

#### View QR Code

In the **Home** tab of the affiliate dashboard, click on the **Share** icon.&#x20;

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot 2026-11-18 144944.png" alt=""><figcaption><p> Affiliate Dashboard > Home</p></figcaption></figure>

Here, click on the **QR code** option.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot 2026-11-18 144948.png" alt=""><figcaption><p>Click on the QR code option</p></figcaption></figure>

After this, the QR code will be displayed. Click on **Download** to save a PNG image file of the QR code for your promotions.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot 2026-11-18 144952.png" alt=""><figcaption><p>QR Code</p></figcaption></figure>

#### Generate QR Codes

You can also generate QR codes for other pages of the store using the product link generator tool in your dashboard.

Go to the **Marketing Tools** section in the affiliate dashboard. In the product link generator tool, paste the product page link. Next, click on the **Share** icon and select the **QR code** option to generate the QR code for that page link.
