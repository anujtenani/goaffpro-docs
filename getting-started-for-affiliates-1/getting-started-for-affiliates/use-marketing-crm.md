# Use Marketing CRM

It is a built-in marketing CRM that helps you manage contacts and leads and send email campaigns directly from your dashboard.

In the Marketing Tools tab, you can go to the Contacts section and click on **View Contacts**.&#x20;

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot 2026-07-15 151341.png" alt=""><figcaption><p>Marketing Tools > Contacts > Click on View Contacts</p></figcaption></figure>

#### View Contacts&#x20;

Here, you can view your existing contacts and their details.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (3914).png" alt=""><figcaption><p>View existing contacts &#x26; their details</p></figcaption></figure>

You also have the option to edit their details, add a note or tag, and send them an email.&#x20;

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot 2026-07-15 151348.png" alt=""><figcaption><p>Edit their details, add a note or tag &#x26; send them an email</p></figcaption></figure>

You can review and edit contact details such as name, email address, phone number, and address.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot 2026-07-15 151447.png" alt=""><figcaption><p>View &#x26; edit contact details</p></figcaption></figure>

You also have the option to create new contacts.&#x20;

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot 2026-07-15 151431.png" alt=""><figcaption><p>Create new contacts</p></figcaption></figure>

#### Create Email Campaigns

To send emails to your contacts, click on **Send Message**.&#x20;

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot 2026-07-15 1513482.png" alt=""><figcaption><p>Send Message</p></figcaption></figure>

Now, click on **New Campaign**.&#x20;

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot 2026-07-15 1538212.png" alt=""><figcaption><p>Click on New Campaign</p></figcaption></figure>

Set the campaign name and select the target audience. Next, set the email's subject line and body content.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot 2026-07-15 151450.png" alt=""><figcaption><p>Set the campaign name &#x26; select the target audience > Set the email's subject line &#x26; body content</p></figcaption></figure>

After this, you can set the campaign schedule.&#x20;

{% hint style="info" %}
You can send the email immediately or schedule it for a particular date and time.
{% endhint %}

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot 2026-07-15 180251.png" alt=""><figcaption><p>Set the campaign schedule</p></figcaption></figure>

Finally, click on **Send Now**.&#x20;

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot 2026-07-15 18025142.png" alt=""><figcaption><p>Click on Send Now</p></figcaption></figure>

The campaign will be created.&#x20;

{% hint style="info" %}
You can click on "**View Details**" to see campaign statistics, such as email opening and click tracking.
{% endhint %}

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (3915).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
