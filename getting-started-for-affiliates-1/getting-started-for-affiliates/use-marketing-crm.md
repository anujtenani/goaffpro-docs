# Use Marketing CRM

In the Marketing Tools tab, you can go to the Contacts section. Here, click on View Contacts.&#x20;

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot 2026-07-15 151341.png" alt=""><figcaption><p>Marketing Tools > Contacts > Click on View Contacts</p></figcaption></figure>

Here, you can view your existing contacts and their details.&#x20;

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (3914).png" alt=""><figcaption><p>View existing contacts &#x26; their details</p></figcaption></figure>

You also have the option to edit their details, add a note or tag and send them an email.&#x20;

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot 2026-07-15 151348.png" alt=""><figcaption><p>Edit their details, add a note or tag &#x26; send them an email</p></figcaption></figure>

You can review the contact details like name, email address, phone number and address.&#x20;
