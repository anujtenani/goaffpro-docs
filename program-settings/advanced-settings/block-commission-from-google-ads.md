# Block Commission from Google Ads

**GoAffPro** provides you with the option to block commission for affiliates on sales that get recorded via Google ads.

{% hint style="info" %}
The Block Google Ads feature lets you prevent sales made via Google ad clicks from being recorded by the app.&#x20;
{% endhint %}

To block commission for affiliates on sales that get recorded via Google ads, go to the **Advanced** section in the **Settings** tab of the GoAffPro admin panel.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (3806).png" alt=""><figcaption><p>Settings > Advanced</p></figcaption></figure>

Here, go to the Block Google Ads section.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (3436).png" alt=""><figcaption><p>Block Google Ads</p></figcaption></figure>

Now, select the action.&#x20;

{% hint style="info" %}
You can set it to reject the sale or keep it pending.
{% endhint %}

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot 2026-10-28 21182042.png" alt=""><figcaption><p>Select the action</p></figcaption></figure>

Finally, when the sale is recorded via Google ads, it will be rejected.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (3437).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

<details>

<summary>FAQ</summary>

**Does the "Block Google Ads" feature only block sales coming from Google ad clicks?**&#x20;

In addition to Google ads, it also blocks sales coming from Bing and DuckDuckGo ad clicks.&#x20;

**What do the different actions mean in the "Block Google Ads" option?**

You can choose from five different actions:​

* **No special action:** Lets the program record commissions on ad sales without any restrictions
* **Reject Sales from Affiliate Google ads:** Rejects sales that originate specifically from affiliate Google ads (excluding brand ads).
* **Reject Sales from All Google ads:** Rejects sales that come from all sources, including both affiliate and brand Google ads.
* **Pending Sale:** Keeps the sale in a pending status, allowing the merchant to approve or reject it.
* **Ignore Ad clicks:** Prevents the referral cookie from being set in the browser if the visit occurs through an ad click.

</details>
