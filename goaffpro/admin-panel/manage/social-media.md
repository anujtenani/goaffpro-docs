# Social Media

The **Social Media** tab can be used to set up social media campaign tracking. It allows your affiliates to track the performance of their social media campaigns and posts.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (3990).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

### Create New Campaign

In the Campaigns section, click on **New Campaign** to create new affiliate campaigns.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (3991).png" alt=""><figcaption><p>Campaigns</p></figcaption></figure>

In the **New Campaign** section, set the campaign name, brief, and select the affiliate. &#x20;

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (3992).png" alt=""><figcaption><p>New Campaign</p></figcaption></figure>

On the Campaign page, you can enter the campaign details and monitor its progress:

* **Campaign Details:** Set details such as campaign timeline (start/end date), campaign links, admin note, affiliate note, and gifts.&#x20;
* **Deliverables:** Create a list of deliverables that outlines the tasks or posts expected from the affiliate during their campaign.&#x20;
* **Milestones:** Establish campaign milestones that serve as key checkpoints with specific due dates.&#x20;
* **Campaign Assets:** Upload campaign assets for the affiliate to utilize.&#x20;
* **Messages:** Communicate instantly with the affiliate through the messaging option.
* **Campaign Stats:** Set a fixed fee, payable to the affiliate for the campaign. View the total commission earnings, payout amount, number of sales, and revenue generated. You can also set the cost of goods percentage to see the gross profit, net profit, and ROI for the campaign.

You can click on the "**View Campaign Page**" option to share the campaign link with the affiliate.

### Creator Lens

In the **Creator Lens** section, you can develop a personal influencer database. This tool allows you to explore influencer profiles, gather relevant data, and then our AI analyzes it to provide a refined, ready-to-use list.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (3995).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

{% hint style="info" %}
To use this feature, you need to install the [Creator Lens browser extension](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/jobimhenojndjkbelalfdlfjppaoncbf?utm_source=item-share-cb) in your Chrome browser.
{% endhint %}
