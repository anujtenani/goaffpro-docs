# Recalculate Commission

**GoAffPro** provides you with the option to recalculate commission on sales.

To recalculate commission on sales, go to the **Sales** tab in the GoAffPro admin panel.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image.png" alt=""><figcaption><p>Sales</p></figcaption></figure>

Here, click on the **order number**.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot 2026-10-14 174210412.png" alt=""><figcaption><p>Click on the order number</p></figcaption></figure>

This will open up the order details window.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (2).png" alt=""><figcaption><p>Order details</p></figcaption></figure>

Now, click on **Recalculate Commission**.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot 2026-10-14 174214215.png" alt=""><figcaption><p>Click on Recalculate Commission</p></figcaption></figure>

Finally, click on **Update**.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot 2026-10-14 174221124.png" alt=""><figcaption><p>Click on Update</p></figcaption></figure>

The commission of the sale will be updated.&#x20;

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (1).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
