# Re-Assign Order to Affiliate

**GoAffPro** provides you with the option to re-assign the order to another affiliate.&#x20;

To re-assign the order to another affiliate, go to the **Sales** tab in the GoAffPro admin panel.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (3986).png" alt=""><figcaption><p>Sales</p></figcaption></figure>

Here, click on the **order number**.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot 2026-10-14 225706.png" alt=""><figcaption><p>Click on the order number</p></figcaption></figure>

This will open up the order details window.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (3988).png" alt=""><figcaption><p>Order details</p></figcaption></figure>

Now, click on **Re-Assign order**.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot 2026-10-14 225710.png" alt=""><figcaption><p>Click on Re-Assign order</p></figcaption></figure>

After this, select the affiliate.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot 2026-10-14 225714.png" alt=""><figcaption><p>Select the affiliate</p></figcaption></figure>

Finally, click on **Submit**.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot 2026-10-14 174221124 (1).png" alt=""><figcaption><p>Click on Submit</p></figcaption></figure>

The order will get re-assigned to the other affiliate.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (3987).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
