# Accept/Reject Affiliate Sales

**GoAffPro** provides you with the option to accept or reject affiliate sales.&#x20;

{% hint style="info" %}
You have the option to accept or reject an affiliate sale. Affiliates will earn a commission only after their sale is approved.
{% endhint %}

To accept or reject an affiliate sale, go to the **Sales** tab in the GoAffPro admin panel.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (3983).png" alt=""><figcaption><p>Sales</p></figcaption></figure>

#### Accept Sales

Here, click on **Accept** to approve an affiliate sale.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot 2026-10-08 03161442.png" alt=""><figcaption><p>Click on Accept</p></figcaption></figure>

This will approve the affiliate sale and change the status of the sale to approved.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (3984).png" alt=""><figcaption><p>Approved sale</p></figcaption></figure>

#### Reject Sales

You can click on **Reject** to reject an affiliate sal&#x65;**.**

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot 2026-10-08 03161412.png" alt=""><figcaption><p>Click on Reject</p></figcaption></figure>

This will reject the affiliate sale and change the sale status to rejected.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (3985).png" alt=""><figcaption><p>Rejected sale</p></figcaption></figure>

<details>

<summary>FAQ</summary>

**Do I need to manually approve or reject affiliate sales?**

No, by default, GoAffPro automatically approves or rejects affiliate sales based on their payment status in the store.

For example, if a sale's payment is successfully processed, the sale gets approved. If a sale is canceled or refunded, it is automatically rejected.

There is an option to manually approve or reject sales, giving you control over this process.

**Do affiliates see rejected sales in their dashboard?**

Affiliates cannot see rejected sales in their dashboard. They can only view approved sales.&#x20;

</details>
